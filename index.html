Questions & answers

Before your visit.

Can I sell without box and papers?

Yes. Box and papers are optional. A full set increases the price. We buy only original watches.

Is the photo estimate the final price?

No. The estimate within an hour is preliminary. The final price is agreed after inspection with you present at our office.

Where do deals take place?

Only at our office in Canggu, Bali, by appointment. The seller comes to the office. We do not travel to sellers.

When do I get paid?

Payment is made at the deal, after inspection and agreement on the final price.

I am on another island. Can I sell?

Yes, if you fly to Bali. The deal takes one visit to our Canggu office. We do not arrange deals in other cities. Send photos on WhatsApp first.

Which watches do you buy?

Original branded watches worth US$5,000 or more, approximately Rp90 million. For another brand, message us on WhatsApp. We do not buy watches below this threshold.
